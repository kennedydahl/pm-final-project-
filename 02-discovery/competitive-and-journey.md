## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A part-time instructor balancing teaching opportunities with a full-time job and other commitments.

The person responsible for coordinating instructor assignments and maintaining the course schedule. (Myself)
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Goal: Quickly find and request available courses that fit their schedule.

Goal: Fill courses efficiently, avoid conflicting assignments, and ensure instructors are confirmed on time.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Persona 1:
By the time they check their email, their preferred courses may already be taken. With no real-time availability or immediate confirmation, they waste time requesting unavailable courses and waiting for responses.

Persona 2: They spend hours sending individual emails, tracking replies, and resolving situations where multiple instructors request the same course, delaying final schedules.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Workaround: Using email and personal calendars to track course opportunities and availability.

Workaround: Manually managing instructor assignments through individual emails, inbox threads, and a compiled scheduling list.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Persona 1:
Steps they take:
1. Check emails for available teaching opportunities.
2. Reply with their preferred courses, sometimes listing multiple options.
3. Wait for the coordinator to confirm availability.
4. Tentatively block time on their personal calendar while waiting for confirmation.
5. Adjust their schedule if their requested course is already taken.

Persona 2:
Email instructors individually with available courses.



Track responses across multiple email threads.



Compare timestamps when multiple instructors request the same course.



Contact instructors to resolve conflicts and offer alternative courses.



Manually update the official schedule and communicate confirmations or changes.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Persona 1:
Instructors have no way to see real-time availability. They waste time requesting courses that are already filled and risk double-booking themselves while waiting for confirmation.
Persona 2:
Scheduling delays and conflicting assignments can leave courses without confirmed instructors, create errors in student communications, and delay contracts and payments.

- **Your journey map, a shareable link, or the map file you committed (e.g. journey-map.html):** Future State Journey: Instructor Scheduling
Strategy: Velocity — reducing manual work and speeding up course assignments.

Visual timeline:

1. Browse → 2. Request → 3. Approve → 4. Confirm

Instructor discovers courses → Requests a slot → Coordinator approves → Schedule updates
4-Stage Journey
1. Browse Available Courses
- User Action: Instructor views available courses → Finds options that fit their schedule.
- Internal State: System displays current availability → Unavailable courses are clearly marked.
- Pain Point Addressed: No visibility → Eliminates guessing which courses are still open.
2. Request a Course
- User Action: Instructor selects a course → Submits a request instantly.
- Internal State: System records request → Prevents duplicate confirmed assignments.
- Pain Point Addressed: Email back-and-forth → Removes the need to request courses through individual emails.
3. Review & Approve
- User Action: Coordinator reviews requests → Approves an instructor for each course.
- Internal State: System records approval → Prevents conflicting confirmed assignments.
- Pain Point Addressed: Manual email tracking → Reduces administrative work and scheduling errors.
4. Confirm & Update
- User Action: Instructor receives confirmation → Can finalize their personal schedule.
- Internal State: System updates course availability → Everyone sees the confirmed assignment.
- Pain Point Addressed: Delayed confirmations → Reduces double-bookings and uncertainty.
3 Competitive Advantages Over Manual Scheduling
1. Real-Time Visibility: Instructors see current availability instead of relying on outdated emails.
2. Faster Assignments: Self-service requests and centralized approvals reduce manual coordination.
3. Greater Accuracy: Confirmed assignments are tracked centrally, reducing double-bookings and scheduling errors.
Business value: Faster, more reliable instructor assignments reduce administrative effort and the risk of unfilled courses and lost revenue.
